DoseGrid

An iOS medication schedule for people taking a lot of them — everything due at the same time in one round.

Everything due at eight o'clock is one round you can tick off in a single tap, with your own spacing rules, supply tracking, and a printed medicines list to take to an appointment.

This site hosts the app's support page and legal documents. The same text is bundled inside the app and readable without a network.
