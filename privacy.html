Privacy Policy — DoseGrid

Last updated: 9 September 2026

The short version

DoseGrid does not collect anything. Your medicines and your dose history stay on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Who is responsible

DoseGrid is made by an independent developer, William Scott, who is the data controller for the purposes of the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation. Because nothing you enter ever leaves your device, there is in practice no personal data for anyone to hold, transfer or lose.

Contact: privacy@dosegrid.app

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — medicine names, strengths, schedules, notes, spacing rules, supply counts and the record of doses taken, skipped or missed — is stored in a private container on your device that is shared only between DoseGrid and its widgets.

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, that container is included in your own encrypted device backup, in the same way as any other app's data. Nobody but you can read it, including us.

What leaves your device

Nothing that you enter.

The only network activity is Apple's own: DoseGrid asks the App Store for subscription pricing and status. That exchange is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your Apple Account, your payment details or your name.

Health information

The medicines you record are health information, and some laws treat it as a special category of personal data. DoseGrid handles it in the only way that removes the risk entirely: it never transmits it. There is no legal basis to declare for a transfer that does not happen, and no third party to name.

DoseGrid does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not ask for permission to.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They are never sent through a server, and their contents never leave your device.

Tracking and advertising

DoseGrid contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and never asks for tracking permission. Its App Store privacy manifest declares no collected data types, which you can verify on the App Store listing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Because your data never leaves your device, you exercise all of them directly and immediately:

  • Access and portability. Settings → History → share the printed list or

the CSV export.

  • Rectification. Edit any medicine or recorded dose in the app.
  • Erasure. Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app.

There is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or delete on your behalf, and no request to us is necessary. You may still complain to your local supervisory authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

California residents

We do not collect, sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

Children

DoseGrid is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all data removes every medicine and every recorded dose from the device immediately. Deleting the app removes the shared container with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published here and bundled in the next release of the app. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
