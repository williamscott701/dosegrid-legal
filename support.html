Support

DoseGrid is made by one person. Email williamscott701@gmail.com and you will get a reply, usually within two working days.

It helps to say which iOS version you are on and what you were doing at the time. Please do not send your medicines list by email — nothing about your schedule is visible from this end.

How it works

Everything due at roughly the same time becomes one round. Eight medicines four times a day is four rounds, not thirty-two reminders, and a round can be ticked off in a single tap. You get one notification per round.

Where your data is

Every medicine and every recorded dose is stored on your device. There is no account and no server, so there is nothing to sign into and nothing to recover remotely. Deleting the app deletes the record with it — export first if you want to keep it.

Common questions

  • A reminder didn't arrive. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → DoseGrid, and that a Focus mode is not silencing it. Reminders are scheduled a fortnight ahead and refresh when you open the app.
  • A dose says "missed" but I took it. Walk back to that day with the arrow at the top left and tick it. A dose only counts as missed after the grace period in Settings, which you can change.
  • Two medicines are in the wrong round. Times within twenty minutes of each other are grouped together. Move one further apart in the medicine's schedule to split them.
  • I got a spacing warning. It came from a rule you entered. DoseGrid has no drug database and never works these out on its own.
  • Moving to a new phone. An encrypted iPhone backup, or an iCloud device backup, carries everything across.

DoseGrid Pro

The free version is a complete schedule for up to four medicines, and is not time-limited. Pro is one annual subscription that adds unlimited medicines, the printed medicines list, supply tracking, ninety days of history and every widget.

  • Restore a purchase. The Pro screen → Restore, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Cancel. iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DoseGrid. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps access until the period ends.
  • Refunds. Apple handles these, not us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What this app is not

DoseGrid is a record of the schedule you enter. It is not a medical device, it has no drug database, it does not check for interactions, and it never recommends a medicine or a dose. Decisions about your treatment belong with your doctor or pharmacist.

If something in the app is wrong, say so in that email. A schedule that shows the wrong thing is worth fixing quickly.
