Terms of Use — DoseGrid

Last updated: 9 September 2026

1. What DoseGrid is

DoseGrid is a personal record-keeping tool. It stores the medicines and times you enter, groups them into rounds, reminds you about them, and keeps a record of what was taken.

2. What DoseGrid is not

DoseGrid is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

It has no drug database. It does not know what any medicine is, what it is for, what it interacts with, or whether a dose is appropriate for you. It cannot detect a wrong dose, a duplicate medicine or a dangerous combination.

Any spacing warning you see comes from a rule you entered yourself. If you did not enter a rule, no warning will appear, and the absence of a warning means nothing at all.

Never start, stop or change a medicine or a dose because of anything this app shows. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist. If DoseGrid disagrees with your prescription, your prescription is right.

Do not rely on DoseGrid in an emergency. Contact your local emergency number.

3. Accuracy is yours

You are responsible for what you enter and for keeping it current. Reminders depend on your device: notifications can be delayed or missed if the device is off, silenced, out of battery, in Focus, low on storage, or if permission has been withdrawn. Treat a reminder as a convenience, never as a safety net.
